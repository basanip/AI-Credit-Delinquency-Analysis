# Business Problem

## Background
Geldium is a financial services company that manages customer credit accounts. The company wants to better understand customer payment behavior and identify customers who may be at risk of becoming delinquent.

## Business Problem
Credit delinquency can lead to financial losses and increased collection costs. Identifying customers only after they miss payments limits the company's ability to intervene early.

The goal of this project is to explore customer and financial data to identify patterns that may indicate an increased risk of delinquency.

## Why It Matters
Early identification of higher-risk customers can help the business take proactive action, improve customer support, reduce financial risk, and make better data-driven decisions.

## Project Objective
The objective is to analyze available customer data, identify important risk indicators, and explore how data analytics and AI can support delinquency-risk assessment.

## Expected Outcome
The project aims to produce:

- Clear insights into factors associated with delinquency risk
- Identification of useful customer and financial patterns
- Data-driven recommendations for potential early intervention
- A foundation for predictive risk analysis
- Practical business insights that can support decision-making
