# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Plan

## Purpose

The purpose of this analysis is to understand the customer and financial data available for the credit delinquency project and identify patterns that may help explain delinquency risk.

## Key Questions

1. What customer and financial information is available in the dataset?
2. Are there missing, duplicate, or inconsistent values?
3. What percentage of customers are delinquent?
4. Which customer characteristics appear related to delinquency?
5. Are there patterns in income, debt, credit utilization, payment history, or account activity?
6. Which factors may be useful for predicting future delinquency?

## Data Quality Checks

The analysis will check for:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Incorrect or unusual values
- Inconsistent data formats
- Outliers that may affect the analysis

## Analysis Approach

The exploratory analysis will include:

1. Understanding the dataset structure
2. Reviewing important variables
3. Checking data quality
4. Comparing delinquent and non-delinquent customers
5. Identifying possible risk indicators
6. Creating visualizations to explain important patterns

## Expected Outcome

The EDA will help identify important patterns and potential risk factors that can be used in later stages of the project for predictive analysis and business recommendations.
